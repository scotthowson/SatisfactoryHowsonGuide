About

The positions of 7,673 things come from the Satisfactory Calculator map data for the base game: 459 ore nodes, 149 wells and geysers, 1,242 Power Slugs, 298 Mercer Spheres, 106 Somersloops, 118 crash sites and 5,301 plants. Satisfactory Plus renames six ores and keeps the nodes where they are. What a crash site asks for is the base game's answer.

Live data comes from the Howson Map Link mod on this PC, at 127.0.0.1:8765. Joined to a dedicated server, the game gives a player no list of what is collected, so the map uses what the mod itself saw and what you tick by hand. Ticks and pins are kept in this browser.
