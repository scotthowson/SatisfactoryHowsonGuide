First session

The first evening of building, for the two of you: who does what first, and what stands by the end. Then Scott's own jobs, which start the factory; his friend's are on First jobs.

Written by tools/startup.py from the plan and from what the server showed on 16 Nov, 23:04; the Satisfactory Plus guide and the World map carry the same steps with tick boxes.

Where the base stands

  • No power. The server counted 0 MW, and seventeen machines with no Power Line: the twelve of Hall 1 and the five of the copper patch.
  • No miner on Iron A, and almost no belts in Hall 1: its machines stand where the plan has them, with nothing between them.
  • Crushed ore in the boxes, no parts: 1,996 Crushed Iron, 958 Crushed Siterite, 660 Crushed Tin, 600 Crushed Gangue, 213 Copper Ingot, 6,625 Crushed Larrussite.

By the end of the evening

  • Power: 120 MW from the burner station, fed from one box, and a line to every machine of both yards.
  • From the boxes: about 2,954 Iron Ingots and 660 Tin Ingots, and the plates, rods, screws and Concrete you made of them.
  • Hall 1: 45 Iron Ingot, 30 Tin Ingot and 15 Concrete a minute, at half speed.
  • The Foundation yard: its floor, and 30 Iron Ingot and 12.5 Crushed Gangue a minute into two boxes.
  • Not yet: the copper patch on belts, Wind Turbines, the Parts hall. Each row above leaves something running, so stop after any of them.

Job 1: Power on

  1. The burner station: four Biomass Burners, one fuel store, one belt. Nothing in the base makes power: on 16 Nov, 23:04 the server counted 0 MW, and seventeen machines without a Power Line. The station goes on the east end of Hall 1's floor, which is laid and empty: two rows of two burners facing one aisle, their middles at -1701, -343; -1684, -343; -1701, -352; -1684, -352, each with its belt inlet toward the aisle. A Storage Container at the aisle's south end, its middle at -1692, -330, outlet to the north; from it a belt north up the aisle with a Conveyor Splitter between each pair, at -1692, -343 and -1692, -352, and a short arm into each burner. Two Power Poles between the pairs, at -1695.5, -347.5 and -1689.5, -347.5: each takes the two burners of its row, and the east one a line to your own pole at -1687.5, -320.3. Four burners give 120 MW. All of it costs 45 Iron Rod, 40 Cable, 35 Iron Plate, 19 Screws, 16 Copper Busbars, 8 Wire, 4 Iron Sheet, 2 Copper Rod. Fill the store with Solid Biofuel or Biomass and all four are fed: full of Solid Biofuel it runs both yards flat out for about 7 hours. The pack has no Biomass Burner Mk.2; this is the one there is. On the map
  2. Hall 1: seven Power Poles and a line to each of its twelve machines. From your pole at -1687.5, -320.3 one line to the hall's first pole, at -1707.5, -316.5; then from pole to pole as the map draws them, each in the 1 m gap beside the machines it serves, one or two machines to a pole. Seven poles cost 28 Wire, 14 Screws, 7 Copper Rod. The hall can ask for 59 MW with everything running at once. On the map
  3. The copper patch: three Power Poles and a line to each of its five machines. Your pole at -1533.2, -304 has one place free: a line from it to the patch's first pole, at -1517.5, -323.5, 25 m. Then the other two poles and the lines to the Crusher, the Constructor and the three Smelters. With both yards and the three miners flat out the station is asked for 86.8 of its 120 MW, and 90.8 with the Crusher by the Elevator pad. You hold 6,625 Crushed Larrussite and nothing uses it before Tier 2's Advanced Sorting, so that Crusher can stay on standby and save the fuel. On the map

Job 2: Parts before any belt

  1. Ingots without a belt: feed Hall 1's Smelters by hand. Your boxes hold 1,996 Crushed Iron and 660 Crushed Tin, and a Smelter with power turns them into ingots whether a belt feeds it or you do. Set the Smelters to Iron Ingot and put a stack into each: one makes 30 a minute, so all seven clear the Crushed Iron in about 10 minutes. Then Tin Ingot for the Crushed Tin, 15 a minute each. The 958 Crushed Siterite smelts as Impure Iron Ingot, one for one. That is about 2,954 Iron Ingots and 660 Tin Ingots before a single belt is laid: more than both tracks' first jobs cost. On the map
  2. Parts without a belt: the three Constructors. Hall 1's two Constructors and the copper patch's one do the same for parts. One on Iron Plate: 30 ingots a minute become 20 plates. One on Iron Rod, 15 a minute, and then on Screws: 20 rods become 50. One on Concrete: your 600 Crushed Gangue make 200. Your friend's first job costs 86 Iron Plate, 76 Concrete, 71 Screws, 37 Iron Rod, 28 Iron Sheet, 22 Cable and smaller parts; in ingots and stone that is about 253 Iron Ingot, 76 Concrete, 31 Copper Ingot, 8 Tin Ingot. Set each Constructor back to Concrete when you are done: that is what the plan has them make. On the map

Job 3: Hall 1 on belts, at half speed

  1. A Modular Miner on Iron A, and one belt to Hall 1. No miner stands on Iron A, at -1704, -283. Set one down with its Standard Mining Head, and a Power Line to it from your pole at -1693.6, -297.3, which has three places free, about 18 m. Then ONE Conveyor Belt Mk.1 from one of its two outlets along the way the map draws: north to Main Street, west along it and into Hall 1 at its south-west corner, -1758.5, -312, 84 m. One outlet is 60 ore a minute, which is the hall at half speed. The plan's full speed joins both outlets on a Conveyor Belt Mk.2, and every piece of that costs 1 Smooth Belt Drive, 1 Iron Sheet; a Smooth Belt Drive is made of Tinned Sheet and Rotors. That waits until Rotors are easy. On the map
  2. Hall 1's own belts: eight of them, 7 Splitters and 9 Mergers. The machines stand; almost nothing joins them. The map draws all eight belts with their letters, and each Splitter and Merger where it stands with what passes through it: 636 m of belt in all. Lay every one as a Conveyor Belt Mk.1 for now. At full speed 198 m of them carry more than 60 a minute and want Mk.2; at half speed none does, and a Mk.1 belt is replaced in place later. Lay them in the order the parts travel: A and B to the Crushers and the Sorter, then C, G and H off them, then D, E and F out. Nothing runs for long until all eight are down, because a Crusher and the Sorter stop as soon as one of their outlets cannot give. 16 Splitters and Mergers cost 80 Iron Plate, 32 Iron Sheet, 80 Screws. On the map
  3. Where Hall 1's three products land. Concrete goes where the plan sends it: from the hall's south-west corner west along Main Street into bay 1 of the Storage depot, 123 m. Nothing in the plan takes the ingots until the Parts hall stands, so end the Iron Ingot belt and the Tin Ingot belt each in a Storage Container of your own by that corner; the plan has no place for those two, and they move when the Parts hall is fed. At half speed the hall then gives 45 Iron Ingot, 30 Tin Ingot and 15 Concrete a minute. The Sorter stops when any of its three outlets cannot give, so all three ends have to be open. On the map

Job 4: The copper patch through its Crusher

  1. The copper patch through its Crusher. Your ore belt runs from the miner straight to the three Smelters, and nothing takes their ingots away. The plan takes the ore to the Crusher at the west end first, never to the Smelters: move the belt's end to the Crusher's inlet. Then the patch's own five belts as the map draws them, 248 m with 2 Splitters and 2 Mergers: crushed Callanite to the three Smelters, stone to the Constructor. Until the long belts to the Parts hall are built, end the Copper Ingots and the Concrete each in a Storage Container at the patch's north-west corner. That is 60 Copper Ingot and 8.33 Concrete a minute. On the map

Job 5: Wind

  1. Wind Turbines on the plateau, from -1736, -216. A Wind Turbine Mk1 gives 20 to 60 MW with no fuel, more the higher it stands, and each one lets a burner rest. One costs 5 Rotor, 15 Iron Rod, 20 Tin Plate, 50 Screws; made by hand that is about 16 Copper Ingot, 77 Iron Ingot, 15 Tin Ingot. With Hall 1 running, the iron for one comes off the belt in two minutes; the Rotors are the handwork. The pads are on the plateau south of the HUB, and the plan's line comes down its north face to the pole at -1745, -310.5 on Main Street. What a turbine gives up there has not been measured: its window says. On the map

Job 6: The Parts hall

  1. The Parts hall, on the floor from First jobs. The next hall of the build order, and the first that makes parts by itself: 12.5 Iron Plate, 5 Iron Rod, 25 Screws, 15 Tin Plate, 15 Cable a minute. Its floor, 6 by 8 Foundations from -1648, -440, is job 4 of First jobs; lay it yourself if you get there first. 6 Constructor and 1 Assembler, 551 m of belt in the hall, and its ingots come up the lane from the two yards. The Assembler costs Rotors; so does every Wind Turbine. Hall plan 5 has every position. On the map

Known and not known

  • The Stove cooks in this build but stops after the server restarts; that is mended in a build that waits for the last install. Do not plan the evening's food round it.
  • A Biomass Burner Mk.2 is not in this pack. The game's own list has one Biomass Burner, the HUB's two, and nothing else that burns biomass before Tier 3.
  • What the HUB's own two burners give is not in the data. The station's four are counted without them.
  • A belt's price by the metre is not known here. The base game charges it once for every 2 m; if the pack keeps that, Hall 1's belts, the ore belt and the Concrete belt come to about 430 Iron Plate and 430 Screws.
  • Seen in the server's own documents, not in the game what stands, what has no Power Line and what the boxes hold, on 16 Nov, 23:04. If any of it has changed since, the map shows it live.
