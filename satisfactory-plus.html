What is different

nine things that are not like the base game
  • The ores are minerals with new names. Siterite holds iron and tin, Callanite copper and magnesium, Larrussite zinc and silica. Later come Aurovite (caterium), Montanion (cobalt and aluminium) and Kyalite (quartz and salt). In the game data they are the base game's iron, copper, limestone, caterium, bauxite and quartz nodes under new names, so the map you know still applies.
  • Ore is crushed before it is smelted, by hand at first, then in the Crusher. From Tier 1 the Sorter splits the crushed ore further: 24 Crushed Siterite become 18 Crushed Iron, 12 Crushed Tin and 8 Crushed Gangue.
  • Concrete is made from Crushed Gangue, the stone that crushing and sorting leave behind. Three make one Concrete.
  • Tin and bronze come before steel. Tin Plates arrive at HUB Upgrade 4, bronze in Tier 2; steel waits for .
  • The miner is modular. A Modular Miner needs a Mining Head: the Standard head gives 30 a minute from each of two outputs, the Hardened head 60, the Diamond head 120. An impure node halves that, a pure node doubles it.
  • No Splitters or Mergers until Tier 1's Logistics Mk. 2. The first lines are straight: one belt, one machine after another.
  • The first power you build is the Biomass Burner (30 MW) or the Wind Turbine Mk1, both at HUB Upgrade 6. The turbine gives 20 to 60 MW depending on how high it stands, and less when other turbines are close.
  • The Space Elevator has a price of its own: 25 Bronze Frame, 75 Modular Frame, 200 Tin Plate, 500 Concrete and 500 Screws. Its first delivery is 75 Smart Plating and 25 Modular Frame.
  • The early game is hand-crafted more than the base game. Tin Plates and Reinforced Iron Plates have no machine until the Assembler in Tier 1.

Tiers 3 and 4: the Lake Works

Tiers 3 and 4: the two-deck works in the lake

The first delivery opens Tiers 3 and 4 together: caterium, molten metal, brass, steel and the Manufacturer. The second delivery asks for 500 Smart Plating, 500 Versatile Framework and 250 Automated Wiring. The Platform works already makes the Smart Plating at 2.25 a minute, so that sets the clock: 222 minutes. Everything here is sized to be ready when the Smart Plating is, the delivery and every part the two tiers cost.

It is one works, and it stands in the lake. The lake is the largest level place beside the camp: 152 by 120 m of open water 16 m north of Hall 1. Two decks of Foundations, one above the other, each 19 by 15: the Foundry on top under the open sky, the Dock works below it. Ore goes up, metal comes down, parts go out. The water the Reformers need is under the floor, and the Air Collector, which collects more the higher it stands, is on the highest floor of the site. Two causeways join it to the camp and to the north shore, so the works is also the bridge across the lake.

How it flows

  1. UpOre crosses the south causeway and rises. Three belts come from the camp over the south causeway and up the lift wall to the upper deck: 62.2 Siterite Ore a minute from Iron B, 36.7 Callanite Ore from the copper miner's second output and 14.2 Larrussite Ore from the west node. Coal comes from the bunker on the gallery, 10.9 a minute; water up the west wall from the lake, 9.35 m³; air from the collector, 7.03 m³.
  2. Upper deckThe Foundry crushes, sorts, melts and casts. 24 machines, 69.7 MW. Four Flexible Blast Furnaces turn crushed iron, copper and zinc into molten iron, steel and brass; three Reformers cast them into Steel Rods, Steel Ingots and Brass Ingots; Smelters make the iron, tin, copper and zinc ingots the yards cannot cover.
  3. DownMetal goes down the lift wall. 9 belts drop 16 m to the lower deck: 5.97 Brass Ingot, 27 Copper Ingot, 31.1 Tin Ingot, 5 Concrete, 17.5 Iron Ingot, 21.1 Steel Ingot, 3.56 Zinc Ingot, 14.2 Steel Rod and 8.33 Sand.
  4. Lower deckThe Dock works presses, assembles and manufactures. 31 machines, 64.9 MW. 15 products: the Versatile Framework and Automated Wiring for the delivery, and 13 kinds of parts for the kit below.
  5. OutParts leave over the south causeway. Down the bank beside Hall 1 and west along Main Street to the Storage depot. The two delivery parts go on to the Elevator pad by hand.
  6. NorthCaterium comes over the north causeway. The Caterium bench stands on the north shore: 7 machines, 2.4 MW, fed by a belt from the Aurovite node on the hill. It makes 10 Quickwire and 1 Caterium Heatsink a minute, which the furnaces, the Reformers and three milestones cost.

Not known until you try

  • Whether Satisfactory Plus lets Foundations stand over open water exactly as the base game does. If it does not, tell me and the works move to the West Field.
  • How deep the lake is. The Mini Water Extractor needs water under it; the map shows open water where it stands, not the depth.
  • The height of the walls you need. The tallest machine on the lower deck is the Manufacturer, 11 m in the data, so a 16 m storey clears it; the Reformer above is 12 m and the Air Collector 28 m, both under open sky.
  • One square of the lake under the decks, at -1776, -448, shows a shoal on the map. If rock comes up through the lower deck there, a Constructor can move.
  • Which side the ports are on, and where the pipe ports are on a Flexible Blast Furnace and a Reformer. The plans put inlets on one long side and outlets on the other.
  • Whether a full AWESOME Sink line behaves as described. It does in the base game.
