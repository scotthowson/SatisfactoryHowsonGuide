# Game plan: Rocky Desert

Written on the assumption that "the sand rocky world" is the **Rocky Desert** starting area. If you meant the Dune Desert (the big sand dunes in the east), say so: it has more ore but little water and few plants, and the early power steps below would change.

The aim: play the base game from the start, build factories that look good, and bring the extra mod content in one branch at a time so nobody is buried.

## What we know about the Rocky Desert

From the Satisfactory wiki:

- West side of the map. The game recommends it for first-time pioneers: flat ground (4 of 5), a good spread of resource types (4 of 5), medium amounts of ore and plants.
- Close to the sea, so water for coal power is easy.
- A long cave with five entrances holds three Quartz nodes and one SAM node. Caves have Stingers.
- The map's highest point, a rock pillar to the north, has one impure Uranium node on top.

Where to base, with every node position and the floors drawn on the surveyed ground, is in [Rocky Desert Works](rocky-desert-works.html). How to build each block is in the [Builder's Handbook](builders-handbook.html).

## House rules

1. **Make it work, then make it nice.** Every production line gets a floor first, and walls and a roof once it runs. No machine sits on bare ground.
2. **One style.** Pick the look at the start and keep to it: for example concrete and FICSIT orange with glass, using Architecture+ and Structural Solutions. Set the paint swatches once; the swatch mod needs the same palette on the server and both PCs.
3. **One extra branch at a time.** The mods add six branches of extra content. Open them in the order in the table below, and only when the base-game step beside them is done.
4. **One base, on a grid.** Foundations on the world grid from the first hall onward, so everything lines up later. Roads, belts and pipes get their own lanes.
5. **Nothing is updated mid-week.** Mods and game change together, server first (see the install guides).
6. **Keep the pace.** No advanced game settings, no unlock commands, hand crafting capped, Industrial Evolution held back until phase 2 is delivered. The reasons are in [Keeping the pace](pacing.md).

## Before the first session

1. Server and both PCs installed from this repo; both of you can join.
2. Create the world on the server with Rocky Desert as the start, mod settings at their defaults.
3. Ten-minute check before building anything real:
   - Place one piece each from Blueprint Designer+, Modern Railing Extension and Really Simple Signs 2. Their pages say multiplayer is not tested.
   - Open the build menu and look for anything named oddly. Architecture+'s own page shows six foundations spelled "Fondation".
   - Note your frame rate standing still at the landing site. That is the number to compare against later.

## The plan

Base-game steps are the tiers and Space Elevator phases as the game presents them. Where a tier number is given for a mod, it is the unlock point from that mod's own page. Where none is given, it is our choice of when to start using it.

| Stage | Base game | Mods to bring in | Build to be proud of |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Landing (Tiers 0 to 2) | HUB, iron, copper and limestone lines, biomass power, first Space Elevator phase | The five starter tools (Smart!, Zoop, Nudge, Move Tool, Curve Builder). Zoop research in the MAM. The small Progressive Blueprinters | Hall 1 and Hall 2 with walls and roofs, the HUB plaza with every bench in its bay, and the storage depot on Main Street |
| 2. Coal and steel (Tiers 3 and 4) | Coal power beside the lake, steel, second Space Elevator phase | Tier 3: Water Dam milestone, the first megaproject site. Tier 4: Power Domain and Modular Load Balancers. Industrial Evolution's MAM research (there from the start; we open it once phase 2 is delivered) | The power station on its terrace over the lake, the Steel Works behind it, and the first hall on the North Shore |
| 3. Oil and rails (Tiers 5 and 6) | Oil, plastic, rubber, fuel power, trains, third Space Elevator phase | Tier 5: Pipe Priorities. Tier 6: Lithium's first item. Start using Modular Stations and Signal Bridge with trains, and Linear Motion's elevators | A train station, and the rail line to the oil site |
| 4. Aluminium and nuclear (Tiers 7 and 8) | Bauxite, aluminium, hover pack, nuclear power, fourth Space Elevator phase | Tier 7: Lithium ore and its Reactive Ore Extractor. Power Domain upgrades, which cost Space Elevator parts | A tower or headquarters; the hover pack makes tall building easy |
| 5. Finish (Tier 9) | The last tier and the final Space Elevator phase | Megaprojects to their last level | Whatever is left on the wish list |

Refined Power and Ficsit Farming are not placed in the table: their pages do not say where they unlock. When their buildings appear in the HUB or the build menu, treat each as its own branch under house rule 3. Farming is a side project for whoever wants it.

## Who does what

Two players, two hats, swap whenever you like:

- **Production:** the next milestone's parts, belts and machine rows.
- **Building:** floors, walls and roofs for what already runs, power, and the next site.

Start each session by agreeing one production goal and one building goal. End it by writing the next two down.
