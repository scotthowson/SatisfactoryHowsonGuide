# Mod guide

A short guide to the mods you will actually touch. The [mod list](mod-list.md) has all 80 with one line each. Keys are each mod's defaults as given on its ficsit.app page; all can be rebound in Options, Keybindings.

The base game's own machines, recipes and milestones are all still there. The mods add tools, building pieces, readouts and extra branches beside them, and the extra branches can be ignored until you want them.

## Start here: five tools for the first evening

| Mod | What it gives you | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smart! | Place a whole grid of foundations, machines or storage in one click; copy a working machine row and continue it | Hold a buildable and press `K` for the Smart Panel. Hold the same building over an existing row and pick a direction to Extend it |
| Infinite Zoop | Zoop walls and foundations in rows and columns, and longer than the base game allows | Works by itself. Longer zoops are researched in the MAM |
| Infinite Nudge | Nudge a locked piece in small steps, rotate it on any axis | With a piece in hand press `H`, then nudge. Left Ctrl is 20 cm, Left Shift 50 cm, Right Shift 250 cm. `Z` changes what the scroll wheel rotates |
| Move Tool | Pick a building up and put it down elsewhere instead of rebuilding it | Left click to pick up, left click to set down, right click to put it back. Ctrl + left click selects several |
| Curve Builder | Curved rows of foundations and walkways | While placing a foundation press `R` and choose Curve. Hold Caps Lock and scroll to set the angle |

## More building tools

| Mod | What it gives you | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Longer Beams | Beams up to 100 m | Shift + scroll changes the length 1 m at a time |
| Construction Preferences | Choose the default pole for belts, pipes and hypertubes, and lift heights | `Alt` + `C` opens the quick switch. With stackable poles as default, hold Ctrl on a pole to stack onto it |
| Pole Position | Places the next stackable pole at the farthest point a belt or pipe can reach, and shows that range as a ring | Hold Shift while placing |
| Persistent Paintables | An eyedropper for colours and materials; buildings keep their colour when upgraded | `Ctrl` + `Q` samples what you point at |
| Progressive Blueprinters | Small blueprint designers from 1 × 1 × 1 foundations upward, early in the game | The first one asks for a full stack of every basic material. A design loads only into a designer of the size it was made in |
| Blueprint Designer+ | Three very large designers: 80 × 80 × 64 m, 96 × 96 × 48 m and 176 × 176 × 96 m | Build inside the frame and save as usual. Its page says multiplayer is not tested |
| Modular Load Balancers | Snap-together balancers of any size | A Tier 4 milestone costing 20 Motors and 20 Modular Frames, then Build Menu, Logistics |

## Building pieces

Twenty-eight mods of walls, floors, roofs, doors, stairs, catwalks, props, lights and signs. They all appear in the normal build menu and the Customizer. The core, in one style that matches the game:

- **Structural Solutions:** frames, concrete shapes, beams, automatic doors and shutters.
- **Architecture+ 01 to 05:** foundations, walls and windows, round windows, doors, glass roofs.
- **Gateways Extension and Spiral Stairs:** stairs, ramps, walkways, catwalks.

Around that: 2 m walls, two extra wall sets, wood and brick walls, a sci-fi wall set, concrete and painted-roof materials, railings, a small roll-up gate, catwalks with ladders, props (factory, industrial, furniture, plants and rocks), lights, power-cable styles, 3D letters and signs.

Three more that help when building:

- **Universal Swatch Slots:** more paint swatches. Everyone must use the same palette as the server.
- **Auto Signs:** a sign placed on a building fills in its own text and icon.
- **All Poles with Indicator Light:** every power pole shows white for fine, red for no power, yellow for a tripped fuse.

## Readouts

| Mod | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Efficiency Checker | Whether a belt or pipe is starved or backing up. Also a hand tool |
| Power Checker | Whether a power grid can blow its fuse |
| Throughput Counter and Limiter | Items per minute on a belt, and an optional cap |
| Production Stats | What the factory makes and uses |
| MiniMap | A small map in the corner |
| Atlas | Your buildings on the big map. On a server it shows only the parts of the world your game has loaded |
| Miner Status on Map | A map marker when a miner or extractor is stuck |
| GameTime and Speedometer | A clock; your speed, position and height |
| Cable Highlighter | `F8` shows nearby power cables through walls |

## Belts, pipes and lifts

Holes through floors for belts, pipes, power and hypertubes, and through walls for belts; more wall supports; covered belts; glass fluid buffers; a small storage box that snaps onto a belt; and **Linear Motion**, which adds elevators.

## Extra content, in the order it turns up

You can ignore all of it and play the base game. Each one is its own branch.

| When | Mod | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| From the start | Industrial Evolution | A research tree in the MAM. Each node gives better versions of machines, which can be upgraded where they stand |
| Tier 3 | Megaprojects: Water Dam | A milestone, "Water Dams and Underground Pipelines". Three fixed sites, named in the mod for the Grass Fields, the Dune Desert and the Titan Forest, so none is near our base. Even an unfinished dam makes 250 MW for free |
| Tier 4 | Megaprojects: Power Domain | After the Expanded Power Infrastructure milestone. One fixed site: a giant battery, 5,000 MW of storage at level 1 and up to 1,000,000 at level 4. Its phases cost Space Elevator parts |
| Tier 5 | Pipe Priorities | Powered pipe splitters and mergers that prefer one pipe over another. Researched in the HUB |
| Tier 6 and 7 | Lithium | A new ore at Tier 7, mined by a Reactive Ore Extractor that burns Packaged Petroleum Ether (Tier 6). Thirteen items, a Jetpack Mk.2. The mod places 22 Lithium nodes in the world; the nearest to our base is at -1610, -539, about 230 m north-east of Hall 1 |
| Not checked | Refined Power | Wind and water turbines, solar panels, a modular boiler-and-turbine power system, reactors. Over 30 buildings |
| Not checked | Ficsit Farming | Fourteen farming buildings |
| AWESOME Shop | Fluid Extras | Extra pipe junction shapes in three shop bundles, a mini water extractor. Its T junction appears twice in the build menu |

"Not checked" means the mod's page does not say where it unlocks. Megaprojects are built once, at a fixed place, and cannot be dismantled.

## Small comforts

Faster hand crafting, mantas that fly above your buildings, lizard doggos you can pick up and carry, and **BoomBox Plus**, which plays the same music for everyone on the server.
