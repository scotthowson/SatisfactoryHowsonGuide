Builder's Handbook

Ten factory blocks you can build anywhere, each drawn on its floor with the numbers that make it work: hand-built blueprints, from a biofuel depot to an oil yard. Build them in order and you have everything through steel, then rotors and oil. Every block is one idea you will reuse for the rest of the game. The arrow in each Splitter and Merger shows which way the flow runs through it. Where they go in our world is in Rocky Desert Works.

Read this first

How to read a drawing

  • You are looking straight down. Each square is one foundation. Columns are numbered, rows are lettered.
  • Everything flows left to right: raw material in on the left, parts out on the right.
  • Click anything. A machine tells you what to set it to, what goes in and out each minute, and which square it sits on. A pole tells you what it powers.
  • Machine sizes are to scale. Exactly where the belt holes sit on a machine is not drawn: point the inlet at the incoming belt.

Keys

  • Q opens the build menu. Type a name to search.
  • Mouse wheel turns the piece before you place it. Left click builds.
  • F dismantles. You get every part back, so mistakes cost nothing.
  • Hold R and choose Zoop to lay a line of foundations in one click.
  • Hold Left Ctrl to lock a foundation to the world grid.
  • H freezes the piece so you can nudge it with the arrow keys.
  • Middle click copies the building you are looking at. E opens a machine.
  • K, with a piece in hand, opens the Smart! panel from the mod pack: a whole row of machines in one click.

What the marks mean

Click a name to light up every one of them on the drawings. Click it again to switch it off.

Three rules

  1. Floor first. Lay the foundations, then the machines, then the belts, then power.
  2. Count per minute. A machine's window shows what it uses and makes each minute. A line works when every machine gets at least what it uses.
  3. Let belts back up. A belt that is full and standing still is working. Each machine takes what it needs and the rest waits.

Words

  • Manifold: one belt running past a row of machines with a Splitter at each. The near machines fill first. It is the simplest way to share a belt and it is what every module here uses.
  • Clock speed: a slider in each machine's window. At 50% a machine makes half as much for less than half the power.
  • Mk.1, Mk.2: mark. A higher mark is a faster version of the same thing.

Module 1 · Basic

Biofuel depot

Six Biomass Burners in a row with one belt feeding all of them. You drop wood or leaves in the container; two Constructors turn it into Solid Biofuel, which burns far longer than raw leaves.

Floor6 by 3 foundations
Machines6 Biomass Burners, 1 Storage Container, 2 Constructors
Power made180 MW at full load
Fuel burned24 Solid Biofuel a minute at full load
The Constructors can make60 a minute, so they idle most of the time
Power used8 MW
Poles4 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 6 by 3 foundations.
  2. Six Biomass Burners along the north edge, side by side, inlets facing south.
  3. Container, then two Constructors, in a line along the south edge. Set the first to Biomass (Wood) or Biomass (Leaves), the second to Solid Biofuel.
  4. One belt from the last Constructor round the east end and west along the burners, with a Splitter in front of each of the first five. The belt ends in the sixth.
  5. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.

Solid Biofuel and the Constructor come in the first HUB upgrades; Foundations with Tier 1, Base Building.

Module 2 · Basic

Smelter pair

One belt of ore, two Smelters, one belt of ingots. A Mk.1 belt carries 60 a minute and one Smelter eats 30, so two Smelters use a belt exactly. Copper is the same module.

Floor3 by 2 foundations
Machines2 Smelters
In60 Iron Ore a minute
Out60 Iron Ingot a minute
Power used8 MW
Poles1 Power Pole Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 3 by 2 foundations.
  2. Two Smelters, inlets facing west, one per foundation row.
  3. Ore belt in from the west with a Splitter in front of the first Smelter; its side outlet feeds the second.
  4. A Merger behind the second Smelter joins both outputs into one belt.
  5. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.
Module 3 · Basic

Plates and rods

The first parts you need by the hundred. Two Smelters feed three Constructors from one belt: the belt backs up until every machine is full, so each takes exactly what it needs.

Floor6 by 4 foundations
Machines2 Smelters, 3 Constructors
In60 Iron Ore a minute
Out20 Iron Plate, 30 Iron Rod a minute
Power used20 MW
Poles3 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 6 by 4 foundations.
  2. Build the Smelter pair at the west end.
  3. Three Constructors in a column east of it: Iron Plate at the top, Iron Rod twice below.
  4. The ingot belt meets a Splitter in front of the middle Constructor; its three outlets feed the three machines.
  5. Plates leave on their own belt. A Merger joins the two rod outputs.
  6. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.
Module 4 · Basic

Wire and cable

Copper works like iron. Four Constructors make Wire; two of them feed one Cable Constructor, the other two fill a container with Wire for building.

Floor8 by 5 foundations
Machines2 Smelters, 5 Constructors
In60 Copper Ore a minute
Out30 Cable, 60 Wire a minute
Power used28 MW
Poles4 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 8 by 5 foundations.
  2. Smelter pair at the west end, set to Copper Ingot.
  3. Four Constructors in a column, all set to Wire. The ingot belt meets a Splitter in front of the second one: one outlet into it, one north to the first, one south.
  4. The south branch meets a second Splitter in front of the third Constructor and ends in the fourth.
  5. Merge the top two Wire outputs into a fifth Constructor set to Cable. Merge the bottom two into a belt to storage.
  6. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.
Module 5 · Advanced

Reinforced Iron Plates

Your first Assembler, and the pattern for every later line: two part chains that meet at one machine. One belt of ore becomes 5 Reinforced Iron Plates a minute with nothing left over. Two machines run at half speed on purpose.

Floor10 by 6 foundations
Machines2 Smelters, 5 Constructors, 1 Assembler
In60 Iron Ore a minute
Out5 Reinforced Iron Plate a minute
Power used38.2 MW
Poles5 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 10 by 6 foundations.
  2. Smelter pair at the west end, 12 m apart this time.
  3. Plate Constructors P1 and P2 in line with the Smelters. S1 feeds P1 directly.
  4. S2 feeds a Splitter: one side to P2, the other south to the Rod Constructor. Each side gets 15.
  5. The Rod belt meets a Splitter feeding two Screw Constructors. Merge the screws and run them north to the Assembler.
  6. Merge the two plate belts into the Assembler's other inlet. Set the Assembler to Reinforced Iron Plate.
  7. Open P2 and Sc2 and set their clock speed to 50%, so they use half the power and never starve the others.
  8. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.

The Assembler comes with Tier 2, Part Assembly.

Module 6 · Advanced

Steel beams and pipes

A Foundry takes two belts at once: 45 iron ore and 45 coal. Two Foundries make 90 steel, which is exactly one Beam Constructor and one Pipe Constructor.

Floor6 by 4 foundations
Machines2 Foundries, 2 Constructors
In90 Iron Ore, 90 Coal a minute
Out15 Steel Beam, 20 Steel Pipe a minute
Power used40 MW
Poles2 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 6 by 4 foundations.
  2. Two Foundries 14 m apart, inlets facing west. Each has two inlets side by side.
  3. Ore belt along the north with a Splitter; coal belt in from the south with a Splitter. Where the coal belt crosses the ore branch, raise it one step on Stackable Conveyor Poles.
  4. Merge the steel and split it again in front of the two Constructors: Steel Beam and Steel Pipe.
  5. The Pipe Constructor fills up first and the Splitter then sends everything else to Beams. That is how 90 becomes 60 and 30.
  6. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.

Steel is Tier 3, Basic Steel Production. Belts faster than 60 a minute are Tier 2, Logistics Mk.2.

Module 7 · Basic

Concrete works

Concrete is what every foundation is made of, so this is the first thing worth automating after iron. Two Constructors share one limestone belt; the second only works part of the time until a better Miner brings more.

Floor3 by 3 foundations
Machines2 Constructors
In60 Limestone a minute
Out20 Concrete a minute
Power used8 MW
Poles1 Power Pole Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 3 by 3 foundations beside a limestone node.
  2. Two Constructors, inlets facing west, both set to Concrete.
  3. The limestone belt comes in at the south-west corner and runs north; a Splitter feeds the second Constructor on the way.
  4. A Merger joins the two outputs into one belt out of the east side.
  5. On a normal node a Miner Mk.1 gives 60 a minute: 20 Concrete. A Miner Mk.2 gives 120 and both Constructors then run flat out: 30 Concrete.
  6. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.

Concrete and the Constructor come in the first HUB upgrades.

Module 8 · Advanced

Steel Works

The steel hall from the Grass Fields plan on a tighter floor: three Foundries, two Beam Constructors and one Pipe Constructor. One belt of ore and one of coal come in on the west side; beams and pipes leave on the east.

Floor6 by 5 foundations
Machines3 Foundries, 3 Constructors
In120 Iron Ore, 120 Coal a minute
Out22.5 Steel Beam, 20 Steel Pipe a minute
Power used57.6 MW
Poles4 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 6 by 5 foundations.
  2. Three Foundries in a column, 13 m apart, inlets facing west. Each has two inlets side by side: ore in the north one, coal in the south one.
  3. Ore belt in at the north-west corner with a Splitter at each of the first two Foundries. Coal belt in at the south-west corner, branching north the same way.
  4. Where a coal branch crosses an ore branch, raise it one step on Stackable Conveyor Poles.
  5. Merge the three steel outputs and split them again in front of three Constructors: Steel Beam, Steel Beam, Steel Pipe.
  6. Set the middle Constructor, B2, to 50%. Leave the Foundries alone: three can melt 135 a minute and one belt brings 120, so each rests a little.
  7. Merge the two beam outputs. Beams and pipes leave the east side on two belts.
  8. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.

Steel is Tier 3, Basic Steel Production. A Mk.2 belt carries 120 a minute: Tier 2, Logistics Mk.2.

Module 9 · Advanced

Rotors

The second Assembler line, and the part Smart Plating is made of. 45 ore a minute becomes 4 Rotors with nothing left over. Every belt is fed the same way: the machines at the end of a line fill up, and the rest goes to the next one.

Floor10 by 5 foundations
Machines2 Smelters, 6 Constructors, 1 Assembler
In45 Iron Ore a minute
Out4 Rotor a minute
Power used42.2 MW
Poles6 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 10 by 5 foundations.
  2. Smelter pair at the west end. Set the second Smelter to 50%.
  3. Three Rod Constructors in a column, fed by one Splitter with all three outlets in use.
  4. A Merger takes all three rod outputs onto one belt of 45. A Splitter sends it north, east and south; a second Splitter on the north branch feeds the top Screw Constructor and lets 20 rods carry on round the top to the Assembler.
  5. Three Screw Constructors in a column; set the bottom one to 50%. One Merger takes all three outputs into the Assembler's other inlet.
  6. Set the Assembler to Rotor.
  7. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.

The Assembler comes with Tier 2, Part Assembly. Rotors are in the same milestone.

Module 10 · Advanced

Oil yard

The oil yard from the Oil Lake plan, drawn for a pure node. Four Refineries make Plastic and Rubber. Both also make Heavy Oil Residue, which two more Refineries turn into Petroleum Coke for an AWESOME Sink, so nothing can ever back up.

Floor13 by 5 foundations
Machines6 Refineries, 1 AWESOME Sink
In120 Crude Oil a minute
Out40 Plastic, 40 Rubber, 180 Petroleum Coke a minute
Power used192 MW
Poles4 Power Poles Mk.1

Build it

  1. Lay 13 by 5 foundations.
  2. Six Refineries side by side, 14 m apart, each with its way in at the north end and its way out at the south end. Each end has one pipe port and one belt port.
  3. Set the first two to Plastic, the next two to Rubber and the last two to Petroleum Coke. Set the last one to 50%.
  4. One crude oil pipe along the north side, with a Pipeline Junction at each of the first four Refineries.
  5. Collect the Heavy Oil Residue from the south ends of those four on one pipe, bring it north up the gap and into the two coke Refineries.
  6. Plastic and Rubber leave the west side on two belts. Coke goes east on one belt into the AWESOME Sink.
  7. An Oil Extractor on a pure node gives 240 a minute. This yard takes 120, so the Extractor rests half the time; a second yard beside it would use the rest.
  8. Power: put the numbered poles where drawn, one between each pair of machines, and chain them in number order.

Tier 5, Oil Processing, unlocks the Oil Extractor, the Refinery and all three recipes. The AWESOME Sink is Tier 2. 180 a minute needs a Mk.3 belt: Tier 4, Logistics Mk.3.

Power, in three steps

StepMachineEach makesBurnsUnlocked
1Biomass Burner30 MW4 Solid Biofuel a minuteHUB upgrades

3Fuel-Powered Generator250 MWFuel made from crude oilTier 5, Oil Processing

Add up the "Power used" lines of what you have built and keep the supply at least a fifth above it. When demand passes supply the fuse trips and everything stops; the Power Checker mod warns you before that.

Poles, neatly: one Power Pole Mk.1 stands in the gap between each two neighbouring machines and powers both. Poles are chained in number order, so every pole carries at most four lines: two machines, the pole before and the pole after.

Make it look good

A hall in four moves

  1. Walls: three rings of 4 m wall on the edge of the floor, 12 m high. That clears every machine in this handbook.
  2. Windows: make the middle ring window walls on the two long sides.
  3. Doors: one gate where belts enter, one where they leave, one door for you.
  4. Roof: flat, one foundation thick, with a light strip down the middle.

Build walls last, when the line inside runs. A hall you cannot walk into is hard to fix.

From the mod pack

  • Architecture+ and Structural Solutions are the core: frames, glass, doors, roofs, beams, in the game's own look.
  • Infinite Zoop lays walls and floors in whole sheets. Curve Builder bends a row of foundations.
  • Move Tool shifts a machine you put in the wrong place.
  • One style: pick a wall, a floor and one accent colour for the whole base, and set the paint swatches once.

What I checked, and what I did not

  • Checked by the script that builds this page: no machine overlaps another or leaves its floor, no pole stands inside a machine or carries more than four lines, and each module's inputs and outputs follow from its machines and the game's recipes.
  • Recipes, machine sizes and power figures are the base game's as of version 1.1. Not re-read against 1.2.
  • Not checked in the game: where the belt holes sit on each machine, so leave yourself a foundation of slack if a belt will not connect.
  • With Industrial Evolution researched, upgraded machines replace several of these one for one. The floors stay the same.
