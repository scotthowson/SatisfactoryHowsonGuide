Rocky Desert Works

Base round the Rocky Desert Lake. A camp between two pure iron nodes, a works across the street from it, a storage row growing west along that street, a power station on the next plain, and the later halls on the north shore, straight across the water. Close together, with a lane between everything: every floor and every piece of ground kept for later fits in 344 by 400 m.

How the base is laid out

  • The Camp, south of Main Street: the HUB and storage yard at the west end, the biofuel depot at the east end, and the pure iron node Iron A between them.
  • The Works, north of Main Street: Hall 1 for iron, Hall 2 for copper, and the Space Elevator alone on its own pad, 49 m from the HUB.
  • The storage row grows west from the HUB along the street, one container at a time, toward the power station. It can never block a hall because it has its own line.
  • The West Plain, phase 2: the power station, on the ground, with four Water Extractors in the lake off its corner and two straight pipes.
  • The North Shore, phases 2 and 3: two blocks of clear ground across the lake, 310 and 280 m from Hall 1, drawn now so they stay empty. A causeway from the power station is marked to reach them.
  • First foundation: north-west corner at -1688, -320. It is the south-east foundation of Hall 1.

Everything on the drawings can be clicked for its position and what to do with it. The site plan can be dragged, zoomed and switched by phase. How each block is built is in the Builder's Handbook.

Three things you asked for

  • More compact: done, where the ground allows. The ground for phases 2 and 3 is on the north shore, 310 m away, not on the larger blocks 540 m and more to the west, so the base stands round one lake. The water pipes run straight. The home floors sit 8 to 24 m apart and cannot close up: the 16 m gap in the Camp has the iron node in it, and the one beside Hall 1 carries the belts.
  • Power station at -1960, -360: blocked. I checked it. Of the 90 squares it would cover, 6 have a rock edge on the map and 30 a contour line; it is the bank down to the lake, falling from -6.6 m to -14 m. The station stays on the nearest block that is clear all over, 24 m east and 56 m south. The outline you asked for is on the plan: click it.
  • HUB further from the Space Elevator: done, sideways. South of the Camp is rock, so the HUB went to the Camp's west end instead: 49 m from the Elevator pad, with the storage row leading away from it.

And one to know

  • Copper is behind a ridge about 35 m high, east of the Works. Its belt has to climb it on Conveyor Lifts or go round its north end. Walk it before you lay the belt.

The storage depot

How it works

  • One part, one container, one sign. Ten are drawn: four on the yard beside the HUB and six along the row.
  • It grows one way. Each new part gets the next bay west, 12 m on. The row's ground is surveyed clear for all nine foundations, and it stops 24 m short of the power station.
  • Belts arrive along the street. Parts come down the lanes from the halls, turn onto Main Street and drop into their container from the street side. Nothing crosses the row.
  • You take from the south side. Walk the back of the row with the HUB at one end: everything you build with is in one line.

Start with eight

One pure coal node with a Miner Mk.1 gives 120 coal a minute, which a Mk.2 belt carries. That feeds exactly eight generators, and eight generators drink exactly three extractors. So build the four western pairs and E1 to E3 first: 600 MW.

Add G5, G10 and E4 when Logistics Mk.3 lets one belt carry 150 and the Miner Mk.2 lets the node give it.

Water goes uphill

The plain is 12 m above the lake and a Water Extractor lifts water only 10 m by itself. One Pipeline Pump Mk.1 on each of the two pipes, at the shore, adds 20 m. Pumps are unlocked with Coal Power, the same milestone as the generators.

Two pipes, not one: a Mk.1 pipe carries 300 m³ a minute and four extractors give 480. Both run straight south from the extractors down the lane east of the station and turn into the aisle; the longer is about 190 m.

Pace and blueprints

What sets the length of the game is the parts each milestone and each Space Elevator phase asks for. No mod in the pack changes those. These are the mods that can shorten the road to them, and what to do about each.

The short version

  • Two settings do most of the work: cap hand crafting, and hold Industrial Evolution back until phase 2 is delivered.
  • Create the world with the game's normal settings. Advanced Game Settings stay off on the server.
  • Nothing here is measured. I have read each mod's own page and its settings file, not timed a playthrough.

Can we use blueprints from the start?

  • Not the downloaded ones. The base game unlocks the Blueprint Designer at Tier 4. The repository holds 194 blueprints; the 15 picked for the early game still need Mk.2 belts or better, all but one.
  • The ImKibitz packs are for later. 179 blueprints made in the Tier 6 and Tier 9 designers. 87 have no belts in them: roads, platforms, walls, walkways and pipe runs. Most of the rest use Mk.5 or Mk.6 belts.
  • Small ones, early. The pack's Progressive Blueprinters adds designers 1, 2 and 3 foundations across. The first asks you to gather a full stack of every basic material.
  • A known snag: that mod's page says saved blueprints will not load back into its three small designers; you build them inside instead.

What I checked, and what I did not

Checked

  • Every floor square against the Satisfactory Calculator map at its deepest zoom, 4.4 pixels to the metre. The page will not build if a floor touches a square with a contour, a rock edge or water in it, if two separate floors are closer than 8 m, or if an extractor is not wholly over deep water.
  • The map's position formula, against a pond whose place was confirmed in the game in the Grass Fields.
  • Node positions and heights against the map's data file. Eleven map heights on and beside the home floors read -7.7 to -5 m.
  • The 22 Lithium nodes the mod adds, read from the mod's own source: none is under a floor. The nearest is 230 m from Hall 1.
  • The two Power Towers stand on clear ground, 140 m apart; the limit is 300.
  • Each hall's line fits its floor exactly, no machine or pole overlaps another, and the recipe arithmetic holds.

Not checked in the game

  • Nobody has stood on this ground. The map is a drawing of the world, not the world. If rock taller than you stands inside a floor outline, stop and tell me the coordinates.
  • Where you land. The game drops you somewhere in the Rocky Desert start area; the walk to the lake is not measured.
  • That four Water Extractors place where drawn. Test them before laying the station floor.
  • The route of the coal belt down the plateau and across the ridge, and the copper belt's way over it.
  • The two North Shore blocks are clear on the map, with one map height beside each: -0.8 and -3.2 m, so 3 to 5 m above the home plain.
  • The causeway is a line on the map, not a design. 112 m of it is over the lake and no rock edge is on it; its two ends are on sloping ground.
  • The map data is from game build 492064; the game is now at 517554. Patch 1.2.1.0 added a limestone node in the Rocky Desert that may not be in it.
  • Anything else the mods place in the world.
